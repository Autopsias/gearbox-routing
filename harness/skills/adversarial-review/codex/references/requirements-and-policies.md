# Requirements and Policies Review

Apply these lenses to requirements, specifications, policies, procedures,
governance rules, and operating processes.

## Scope and Actors

- Identify who must, may, and must not perform each action.
- Check that systems, users, administrators, reviewers, and third parties are
  defined consistently.
- Look for undefined scope across products, regions, environments, or data
  classes.

## Clarity and Consistency

- Find conflicting obligations, permissions, deadlines, and definitions.
- Flag ambiguous terms only when multiple reasonable interpretations produce
  materially different behavior.
- Check references, precedence rules, and exception handling.

## Completeness and Testability

- Verify that requirements state observable outcomes or enforceable rules.
- Check inputs, outputs, preconditions, failure behavior, and acceptance
  criteria.
- Identify requirements that cannot be tested or audited as written.
- Distinguish intentional discretion from accidental vagueness.

## Exceptions and Abuse Cases

- Test emergencies, outages, delegated access, lost credentials, conflicting
  duties, and inaccessible approval paths.
- Look for loopholes, incentive problems, privilege escalation, and selective
  enforcement.
- Check whether exceptions expire, are logged, and receive review.

## Enforcement and Auditability

- Identify the enforcement mechanism, responsible owner, evidence retained, and
  consequence of violation.
- Check whether compliance can be demonstrated without collecting excessive
  data.
- Verify appeals, corrections, and escalation paths where stakeholder impact is
  material.

## Fairness and Stakeholder Impact

- Identify groups bearing cost, delay, risk, or reduced access.
- Check whether proxies or exceptions create disparate outcomes.
- Separate a demonstrated inconsistency from a normative disagreement.

## Compliance Claims

- Treat claims of legal, regulatory, or standards compliance as externally
  factual and time-sensitive.
- Verify the applicable jurisdiction, version, scope, and authoritative text.
- Do not present the review as legal advice or infer compliance from intent.

## Traceability

- Map each rule to an objective, risk, source of truth, or governing obligation.
- Identify objectives with no rule and rules with no stated purpose.
- Check that implementation and audit evidence can trace back to the requirement.
